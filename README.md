# Wifejak
The Wifejak squeezes back.

The "Wifejak" architecture is a hand-held gun (handgun, rifle, shotgun) aimbot designed to give upstanding law-abiding citizens access to cutting edge real-time AI integration to perform ms-scale target image recognition, total user-authority trigger actuation, tactile targeting guidance for "eyes shut" and around-the-corner aiming, computational go-signaling for trigger pull, and open-source 3D printable mechanism designs. Many women (and more men than would care to admit it) are disuaded from carrying a self-defense weapon by the aprehension that when the time came they would be unable to look down the sights and decide to kill another human being, let alone accurately discharge their firearm on-target. The Wifejak addresses this with three connected but independent open-source components.

1. The Camera. Camera availability and firearm variation makes camera mounting for real-time targeting image recognition a difficult task. Wifejak addresses this with a flexible-yet-robust optic mounting system which can adapt any consumer-available camera to any firearm or telescopic optic.

2. The Processor. Users have a wide range of access to processing, from microcontrollers to low-latency server-scale links. Wifejak addresses this with a generic codebase that is scalable and adaptable to the users' needs. Your processor can and should be trained on your gun, with your camera, your actuator, your hand, and your ammo load. Like a real wife, the pairing is deeply personal. An optional biometric lock can be included in the loop to make hijacking more difficult. At the same time, any number and type of cameras can be connected to the processor, allowing targeting to draw on a wide range of feeds in order to make the trigger-release decision.
3. The Actuator. At the end of the day, a human has to pull the trigger. Wifejak never determines IF you pull the trigger, it only gets a say in exactly WHEN. This is because the Wifejak actuator mounts alongside your gun trigger and stays out of your way. If you decide to pull the trigger yourself Wifejak won't (and physically can't) stop you. If, on the other hand, you decide you want Wifejak's assistance, simply slip your finger into Wifejak's hole and gently squeeze. The actuator will pre-load and the processor will pull the release at the right time to deliver the round on-target. An optional tactile guidance feedback can be added to the actuator in order to allow out-of-sight computational aiming feedback (the "squeezes back" part), while leaving where the gun goes under the total authority of the user.

FAQs:


* Q: Can I use the Wifejak with one hand while I use the other to cover one ear with my bicep and the other ear with my fingertips, thereby allowing me to avoid hearing damage in a likely minimly prepared self-defense situation?
 A: Yes, we recommend doing this.
* Q: What if my arm won't reach all the way over my head?
 A: Then you are a baby, and must rely on your mother to protect you. The Wifejak is designed to assist her in this responsibility.
* Q: What if I want to just hold down the trigger and keep shooting?
 A: The ATF has entered the chat! No, the Wifejak actuator is a single-pull mechanism, preloaded by a finger pull and released in a similar way to a bow catch. It is physically impossible for the mechanism to pull the trigger of the gun more than once per pull of the mechanism.
* Q: How much does it cost?
A: The Wifejak project does not create, manufacture, print, or otherwise supply any physical objects, nor does it provide gun-specific geometry. If you want a Wifejak then, just like in real life, you have to meet her in person.
